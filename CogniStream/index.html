Analytics Methodology, Formulas & Ethics

Mathematical documentation of session reconstruction heuristics, interval union deduplication, and metric limitations.

Core Principle: Observable Proxies vs. Mental States

CogniStream does not measure subjective psychological concentration, emotional exhaustion, or individual competence. All calculations measure observable telemetry proxies—specifically uninterrupted active editor input and potential communication triggers. Metrics are aggregated at team levels to diagnose workflow friction (e.g., CI/CD notification noise) rather than surveillance.

1. Session & Focus Block Reconstruction

Developer telemetry is ingested continuously from IDE Activity Watch as discrete heartbeats (every 2–4 minutes of active keyboard/mouse input). To reconstruct coherent sessions from point telemetry:

• 15-Minute Inactivity Threshold: Heartbeats separated by ≤ 15 minutes are merged into the same session. Any gap > 15 minutes splits the stream into a new block.
• Explicit Session Boundaries: Signals like ide_session_started and ide_session_ended override inactivity windows.
• Qualifying Focus Threshold (90 min): Continuous sessions with active duration ≥ 90 minutes qualify as deep focus. Blocks < 90 minutes are classified as fragmented sessions.

2. Non-Overlapping Interruption Interval Union

When multiple interruption events arrive in rapid succession (e.g., three Slack messages within 2 minutes), naively multiplying nominal recovery times would produce artificial double-counting where estimated lost time exceeds total elapsed clock time.

Interval Union Algorithm:

Given interruption events at timestamps \( t_i \) with recovery durations \( d_i \), each event forms interval \( I_i = [t_i, t_i + d_i] \).

The total effective recovery loss is computed via non-overlapping interval union:

Total Loss = Length( ∪ [t_i, t_i + d_i] )

This mathematically guarantees zero double-counting regardless of notification burst intensity.

3. Context-Switching Tax Formulation

The Context-Switching Tax (%) represents the proportion of focus capacity consumed by interruption recovery:

Tax (%) = [ Total Interruption Recovery Minutes / (Observed IDE Active Minutes + Total Interruption Recovery Minutes) ] × 100

Includes defensive zero-denominator safeguards yielding 0.0% if total activity is zero.
